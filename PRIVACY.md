# JimBuddy Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 October 2026_

JimBuddy is a food and strength tracker. It has no account, no server and no ads, and it does not collect or sell your data.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you enter (meals, workouts, weigh-ins, body measurements, targets, progress photos and step counts) is stored only in a database on your device. The developer cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Permissions

- **Camera and photos:** to take or pick meal photos and progress photos. Photos stay on your device.
- **Physical activity (step counter):** to read your step count and add walking calories to your day. Steps stay on your device.
- **Notifications:** for the rest timer between sets. The notifications are scheduled locally on the phone.

JimBuddy does not use your microphone, location or contacts.

## When data leaves your phone

This only happens when you choose it:

- **ChatGPT hand-off.** When you tap a "ChatGPT" button, JimBuddy opens ChatGPT (chatgpt.com or the ChatGPT app) with a prepared prompt. For target suggestions, that prompt includes the body stats you entered, such as age, height, weight and goal. What happens next is covered by [OpenAI's privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy). JimBuddy reads your clipboard only to load a table you copied from ChatGPT.
- **Backups.** When you export a backup, JimBuddy hands the file to your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes.
- **Feedback.** When you send feedback, JimBuddy hands a short message (your name, the option you picked and your note) to your phone's share sheet, and you choose the app and the recipient.

## App updates

JimBuddy downloads app updates from Expo's update service (expo.dev). These requests contain standard technical details, such as the app version and platform, and none of your tracking data.

## Children

JimBuddy is not directed at children under 13.
